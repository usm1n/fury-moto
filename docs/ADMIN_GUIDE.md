# The Gear Doctor: Shop Admin Guide

This guide is for running the shop from your phone. You do not need any tech skills.

Use Chrome or Safari. Keep internet on while you work.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open your browser. Go to **`<your-domain>/admin`**.
2. Type your email and password. Tap **Sign in**.
3. The first time, you must set your own password:
   1. Type the starting password you were given.
   2. Type a new password. Use at least 12 characters, with a capital letter, a small letter and a number.
   3. Type it again. Tap **Save password**.
4. If the password is wrong 5 times, the account locks for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again.
5. You stay signed in for about 8 hours. After that, sign in again.
6. **On a shared phone, always sign out.** Tap **Sign out** at the top right.

---

## 2. Home screen

The home screen shows three things:

- **New orders.** The big box at the top. Tap it to see new orders.
- **Revenue this month** and **Orders this month.**
- **Low stock.** Items that are running low. "Out" means none left. "3 left" means three left. Tap an item to fix its stock.

The bottom bar has five buttons:

- **Home**
- **Orders**
- **Add** (new product)
- **Products**
- **More** (Settings)

---

## 3. Adding a product

Save as you go. Do not try to do everything in one go.

1. Tap **Add** at the bottom.
2. **Details**
   - Type the **Title**. The web address fills in by itself. Leave it alone.
   - Choose the **Brand** and **Category**.
   - Write a **Short description** (one line) and a **Description**. Leave a blank line for a new paragraph.
   - **Gear Doctor note**: your own advice on fit or use.
3. **Pricing**
   - **Price (Rs)**: what the customer pays.
   - **Compare-at price (Rs)**: the "was" price. Fill it in only for a sale. It must be higher than the Price. The shop shows it crossed out.
   - **Badges**: New, Rider's pick, Sale. Pick any that apply.
   - Switch on **Featured** to show it on the home page.
4. **Certifications**: tick the ones that apply.
   - **DOT**: the US minimum helmet standard. The maker certifies it themselves.
   - **ECE 22.06**: the current European helmet safety standard.
   - **FIM**: racing approval for track and circuit racing.
   - **SNELL**: an independent helmet test, stricter than the legal minimum.
   - **CE Level 1 and Level 2**: armour impact standards. Level 2 gives more protection.
5. **Specs**: tap **Add spec**. Type a name (for example "Shell material") and a value (for example "Carbon fibre"). Use the arrows to reorder. Use the bin icon to remove a row.
6. Tap **Save**. On a new product, the button says **Save draft**.
7. **Variants and stock** (after the first save)
   - Type sizes, separated by commas (S, M, L). Tap **+**.
   - Add colours: type the name, pick a colour dot, tap **+**.
   - The grid makes a row for every size and colour. Type the stock for each row.
   - Tap **Save variants**. This is a **separate** button. Press it too.
   - To stop selling one combination, switch it off. Do not delete it.
   - With no sizes or colours, saving creates one "One size / Standard" item.
8. **Photos** (after the first save)
   - Tap **Take photo / Choose photos**. Use the camera, or pick several photos from your gallery.
   - Photos shrink automatically. Wait until each one finishes.
   - The first photo is marked **Main**. It is the one shown first. To change it, drag another photo to the front using the arrows icon.
   - Under each photo, type a short **Alt text** (for example "Front view, matte black"). Tap outside the box to save it.
   - To delete a photo, tap the bin icon, then **Delete**.
9. Tap **Publish**. The product goes live on the shop.

**Draft, Published, Delete**

- **Draft**: saved but hidden from customers. Use this while you finish a product.
- **Published**: live on the shop. You can also switch it on or off from the product list.
- **Delete**: permanently removes the product and its photos. Past orders keep their own copy. You will be asked to confirm first.

**Duplicate** makes a copy as a Draft. Change the title, then check the photos, sizes and stock.

**"Changed elsewhere"**: someone else, or you on another device, changed this product while you were editing. Your unsaved changes would be lost if you reload. Copy your text first. Then tap **Reload** and type the changes again.

## 4. Stock and prices

- **Stock:** open the product. Go to **Variants and stock**. Change the number. Tap **Save variants**.
- **Price:** open the product. Change the price. Tap **Save**.
- Stock goes down when you **Confirm** an order (see section 6).
- A size with zero stock is greyed out on the shop.
- A product with no stock in any size shows as **Sold out**.
- When a size falls to your low-stock number or below, the shop shows **"Only N left"** under it.

---

## 5. Categories and brands

On a phone, type **`<your-domain>/admin/categories`** or **`<your-domain>/admin/brands`** in the browser's address bar. On a computer, use the side menu.

- **Add:** type a name. Tap **Add**.
- **Rename:** tap the pencil. Change the name. Tap the green tick.
- **Order:** drag the handle up or down. This sets the order on the shop.
- **Hide:** switch the toggle off. Use this instead of deleting.
- **Delete:** works only if no product uses it. If you see "is in use", hide it instead.

---

## 6. Orders

**What a new order looks like.** It appears under **New**. You see the order number (GD-1001, GD-1002...), the customer's name, city, total and the time placed.

**How the customer reaches you.** After checkout, the customer taps a WhatsApp button. It opens a message to your WhatsApp with the full order. If their phone blocks it, the message appears on screen with a **Copy** button. They paste it into WhatsApp and send it to you.

**Status flow**

**New → Confirmed → Shipped → Delivered**

You can **Cancel** an order at any stage before it is Delivered.

**Confirm.** Talk to the customer first. Make sure they still want it and agree to the delivery. Then tap **Confirm order**.

- Stock is taken off for every item.
- If there is not enough stock, you see **"Not enough stock to confirm"**. Each item is listed with what was needed and what is left. Either fix the stock (only if the item is really there), or cancel the order, or call the customer about another size or colour.
- This can happen when two customers ordered the last one. Only the first confirmed order gets it.

**Cancel.** Tap **Cancel order**. Add a note if you like. Confirm.

- If the order was already Confirmed, its stock goes back on the shelf.
- If it was still New, no stock was taken, so none comes back.

**Notes.**

- **Add a note** at the bottom of the order. Only you see it. Use it for things like "called, no answer".
- The customer's own notes from checkout show under **Notes**.

**Message the customer.** Tap **Message customer on WhatsApp**. It opens a chat with them.

**Packing slip.** Tap **Copy packing slip** to paste it into a message. Or tap **Print packing slip**. It shows the items, total and payment type.

**Payment**

- **Cash on delivery:** the customer pays cash when the parcel arrives.
- **Bank transfer:** check your bank app for the payment before you ship.

## 7. Settings

Tap **More**, then **Settings**. Tap **Save settings** after every change.

- **WhatsApp number:** digits only, with country code. No + and no spaces. Example: `923001234567`. Order messages go here.
- **Phone, email, city, address, hours:** shown on the website. Example phone: `0300 1234567`.
- **Delivery fee (PKR):** added to every order. Use 0 for free delivery.
- **Low-stock number:** when a size reaches this number or below, the dashboard lists it and the shop shows "Only N left".
- **Cash on delivery** and **Bank transfer:** switch on the ways you accept payment. For bank transfer, fill in the bank name, account title and IBAN.

---

## 8. Good habits

- Check **New** orders every morning and evening.
- Confirm only after you have spoken to the customer.
- Update stock the same day you sell or receive items.
- **Photos:**
  - Shoot in daylight, near a window. Avoid flash.
  - Use a plain background, such as a white wall or grey sheet.
  - Take 4 to 6 photos: front, back, both sides, close-up of details, and the label.
  - Keep the phone steady. Fill the frame with the product.

---

## 9. Troubleshooting

- **Can't sign in.** Check the email spelling and that Caps Lock is off. If you were locked out, wait 15 minutes. Still stuck? Contact your developer (below).
- **Photo won't upload.** Check your internet connection. Tap **Dismiss**, then try that photo again. Upload one photo at a time. If you see "Photo storage is not configured", contact your developer.
- **Change not showing on the website yet.** Wait up to one minute, then reload the shop page.
- **"Changed elsewhere."** See section 3.
- **Who to contact:** **[Developer name and WhatsApp number, to be filled in]**
